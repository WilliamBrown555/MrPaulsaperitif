## Notes

- **Amargo de Chile, not “Chille”** (corrected Sept 2026). And the “Chile” is the pepper, not the country — it’s CH Distillery’s *Amargo de Chile*, made in Chicago. Any menu copy calling it South American is wrong.
- **Both house pours resolved, Sept 2026.** The sambuca is **Romana Sambuca, 42%**. The blackberry is **Leroux Jeżynówka “Polish” Blackberry, 35%** — every bottle on the online menu now carries an ABV.
- **Leroux “Polish” is not Polish.** *Jeżynówka* is the Polish word for a blackberry nalewka, but this is a US-made flavoured brandy — Leroux is owned by Phillips Distilling, and the brand’s own copy says only that it is “seasoned to the Polish taste.” The menu says United States for that reason. Don’t let anyone describe it as imported.
- **No half pours.** Everything here runs $8–20, well under the $25 threshold that triggers a half pour on the whiskey list. A half pour of Fernet isn't a thing anyone orders.
- **Tawny lives on a different Toast menu** — Wine By the Glass → Dessert Wine, not Spirits → Liqueurs/Cordials. Two places to check on a rebuild.
- **Possible duplicate Madeira.** Aperitif carries *H&H Madeira ($10)* from Liqueurs/Cordials; Dessert Wine separately lists *GLS Madiera ($12)*. Might be the same wine entered twice at different pours, might be two products. Worth checking the bottle.
- **No vermouth.** Not in the Toast group. If the bar pours Dolin, Carpano, Cocchi or similar, they belong under Aperitif and need to be added by hand.
- **No Amaro group on Toast.** The category is mixed into Liqueurs/Cordials with cordials, absinthe and aquavit. Creating an Amaro group in Toast would make the next pull clean, but it isn't required.
- **ABV and origin not yet researched.** The whiskey and agave lists both carry them. Say the word and they get filled in the same way.
